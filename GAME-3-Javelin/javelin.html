How to play

  • Angle tilts Rocky's javelin. Pace is how fast he runs up, so how hard he throws.
  • The dotted line shows only the start of the flight. The faint line is your last throw: change a little and try again.
  • Clear every target: hit the boards, pop the balloons, knock the trophy cups to the ground.
  • Just Balloons (on the start screen): pop 4 balloons in as few javelins as you can. They rise into the sky, so aim where a balloon will be when the javelin gets there. One that floats away comes back up somewhere new.
  • Wind (Practice field and Just Balloons): tap the windsock at the top right, or press N, to switch between no wind, light, strong and random wind (a new wind after every throw).
  • Javelins left over at the end earn bonus points and stars.

Javelins

  • Standard: sticks into wood, bounces off stone.
  • Heavy (World 2): smashes stone, but drops sooner.
  • Feather (World 3): flies far, but the wind pushes it a lot.
  • Triple (World 4): press THROW again in the air to split it into three.
