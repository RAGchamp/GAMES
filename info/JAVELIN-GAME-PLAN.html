What is this plan and how does AI play a part?

This page is the build plan for Game 3: Javelin, the third game in the RAG Champ Games portal. Like Car Race and Chintu, it will be written by an AI coding agent (Claude Code) as one HTML file with plain JavaScript and the Canvas API. There are no frameworks, no build step and no server. The game is built from a short idea and one character picture of Rocky. PLEASE NOTE: players don't need any AI to play the game. The AI is used only to write the code.

🎯 Javelin: Development Plan

Plan date: 22 Nov 2026 · Decisions agreed 22 Nov 2026 · Status: All 6 phases done 22 Nov 2026 (play Javelin · how it works)

Goal: Build GAME-3-Javelin/javelin.html, a physics launch-and-topple puzzle game. The player sets two sliders, Angle and Pace, and presses THROW. Rocky runs up and throws his javelin, which flies along a real arc and lands where the physics takes it. Each level has towers of crates, glass and stone with targets on them (bullseye boards, balloons and trophy cups), and Rocky has only a few javelins to knock them all down.
1The story

Rocky is the star javelin thrower of his town's sports club, and he wants to win the Golden Javelin Trophy at the big Sports Day.

His coach has a famous training method: the Target Challenge. On every ground she builds wobbly towers from crates, planks, glass panes and stone blocks, and puts targets on them. Rocky can't climb the towers, but he can throw. Every good throw knocks targets down, and each cleared ground moves him one step closer to Sports Day.

The last stage is the Championship Tower: the tallest tower of all, with the Golden Trophy on top.

Targets only, by design (agreed 22 Nov 2026)
  • There are no villains or characters to hit, only targets. The javelin never hurts anyone.
  • The javelin sticks into wood, boards and ground and bounces off stone and metal, which keeps it a sport.
  • A Practice field lets players just throw for distance, as in the real sport.
2Characters (drawn in code from the reference picture)
Hero
🏃 Rocky
A sporty, determined boy with swept-up light-brown hair, big blue eyes and a focused look. He wears a blue athletics vest, dark-blue shorts with a white side stripe, white wristband and blue-and-white running shoes. His javelin has a wooden grip and a silver tip. Animations: idle bounce, run-up (6 frames, faster with more pace), crossover step, throw, follow-through, cheer and a disappointed head-shake.
Targets
🎯 Boards, balloons, cups
Bullseye boards: the javelin sticks in; a hit nearer the centre scores more. Steel-rim boards: need two hits, or knock them off their tower. Balloons: pop when touched, some float and drift in the wind. Trophy cups: cleared when they fall to the ground. Bells: ring when hit, for bonus points.
Building pieces
Wood, glass, stone
Wood (crates, planks): the javelin sticks in and breaks it after a strong hit. Glass: shatters easily. Stone: heavy, the javelin bounces off unless it is the Heavy javelin.

Rocky will be an original cartoon drawing made with canvas shapes, matching the colours and features of the reference picture. The picture itself is not copied into the game, because it looks like a stock sticker image. The coach appears only as a small portrait with a speech bubble in the cut-scenes.

3How it plays
  • Angle slider: 5° to 85°. Rocky's arm and the javelin in his hand tilt live as the slider moves.
  • Pace slider: how fast Rocky runs up, from a jog to a sprint. Pace sets the release speed of the javelin, so it decides how far it flies. A pace bar fills from green to red.
  • THROW button: Rocky runs from the start mark to the throwing line, plants his foot and throws. The camera follows the javelin and comes back when everything stops moving.
  • Aim guide: a dotted line shows the start of the flight path (the first 25%). On Easy it shows half the path. It never shows where the javelin will land, so finding the right throw is the puzzle.
  • Last-throw ghost: a faint line shows where the previous javelin went, so players can make small changes, the way you learn in the real sport.
  • Limited javelins: usually 3 to 5 per level, shown as a rack beside Rocky.
  • Level cleared when every target is done: boards hit, balloons popped, trophy cups on the ground.
  • Stars: 1 star for clearing; 2 stars for clearing with a javelin left over; 3 stars for 2 or more left, or for a score above the level's target.
  • Score: points for each broken block and each target (more for a bullseye centre), bells rung, and a big bonus for every javelin not used.
  • Wind (from World 2): a windsock and an arrow show the wind's strength and direction, and the wind pushes the javelin sideways in flight.
  • Javelin types (unlocked one per world): Standard; Heavy (iron tip, breaks stone, drops faster); Feather (light, flies far, pushed a lot by wind); Triple (splits into three when you tap during flight).

7Technical design
Game loop
Fixed 1/60 s step
The same accumulator pattern as Car Race and Chintu: update(dt) runs at a fixed rate and render() runs once per frame. The game pauses when the tab is hidden.
The throw
Sliders → velocity
Release speed v = vMin + pace × (vMax − vMin). Starting velocity vx = v·cos(angle), vy = v·sin(angle). Each step: vy −= g·dt, wind and a little air drag change vx. The javelin is drawn pointing along its velocity, so it noses over like a real one.
Landing
Tip-first sticks
The tip is tested against the ground and blocks. Tip-first into ground or wood: it sticks and wobbles. Flat or into stone: it bounces and slides. On contact it passes its momentum to the block, which can push or break it.
Towers
Matter.js rigid bodies
Blocks fall, tip over, stack and break, with a strength value per material; breaking is our own code on top of Matter.js collision events. Bodies start asleep so towers stand still until hit. A level is "settled" when all bodies are still for 1 s, then the next throw is allowed.
Saving
localStorage, guarded
Saves levels unlocked, stars, best scores and the practice personal best. Every read and write is wrapped in try/catch so the game still works in a private window.
Input
Sliders, keys, touch
Real <input type="range"> sliders, big enough for thumbs. ↑/↓ angle · ←/→ pace · Space throw (and split a Triple) · R restart level · P/Esc pause.

9Test checklist
  • Opens from file:/// in Chrome, Edge and Firefox with no console errors.
  • The same slider settings always give the same throw when there is no wind (the physics is repeatable).
  • The javelin never passes through a thin plank or glass pane, even at top speed.
  • Towers stand still until hit; nothing jitters or slowly slides apart.
  • Every level can be cleared, and has been cleared with 3 stars during testing.
  • Switching tabs pauses the game; there are no stuck keys or sliders afterwards.
  • The game still works in a private window (localStorage blocked).
  • Rotating a phone keeps the canvas sharp, and the sliders never cover the towers.
10Decisions (agreed 22 Nov 2026)
  • Physics engine: Matter.js. The open-source Matter.js library (MIT licence) is pasted into the game file and credited in the footer, as Car Race credits its road engine. It handles toppling and stacking; breaking, targets and the javelin's own flight are written by hand.
  • Targets only, no villains. Rocky throws at bullseye boards, balloons, trophy cups and bells on towers set up by his coach for the Target Challenge. The final stage is the Championship Tower instead of a boss.
  • Two sliders: Angle and Pace. The player sets both sliders and then presses THROW, so a throw can be repeated and fine-tuned. There is no hold-to-power mode.
  • Art style: drawn in code. Rocky is an original cartoon character drawn with canvas shapes, based on the colours and look of the reference picture. The picture itself is not used in the game.
  • Genre, not a copy. The game uses the familiar launch-and-topple puzzle idea (limited throws, towers that fall, stars), but has its own hero, targets, levels, art and sounds, and the throw is controlled with sliders instead of a slingshot. No characters, levels, music or artwork from any existing game are used.
