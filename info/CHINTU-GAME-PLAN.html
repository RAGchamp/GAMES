What is this plan and how does AI play a part?

This page is the build plan for Game 2: Chintu, the second game in the RAG Champ Games portal. Like Car Race, it will be written by an AI coding agent (Claude Code) as one HTML file with plain JavaScript and the Canvas API. There are no frameworks, no build step and no server. This time there is no open-source example to start from: the game is built from scratch from a short story idea and two character pictures. PLEASE NOTE: players don't need any AI to play the game. The AI is used only to write the code.

🏃 Chintu: Development Plan

Plan date: 20 Nov 2026 · Decisions agreed 21 Nov 2026 · Status: Phase 1 done (play the test run) · Phase 2 next

Goal: Build GAME-2-Chintu/chintu.html, a side-scrolling platform runner in the classic jump-and-run style. Chintu keeps running to the right, jumping and sliding past hurdles, stomping henchmen and collecting pages of a secret formula. The villain Dr. Andhera watches from his lair and gets more and more worried as Chintu gets closer, sending harder obstacles. The twist: the formula makes people invisible, but anyone invisible can still be seen under red light.
1The story

Chintu is an ordinary, cheerful boy who finds out that he holds the key to a secret formula. One drop of it makes a person invisible. There is only one weakness: red light shows an invisible person as a glowing red outline.

Dr. Andhera wants the formula so his army can never be seen. He has hidden the formula vault inside his fortress, and the vault opens only with the key that Chintu carries. Chintu has to run across the town to the fortress, collect the scattered formula pages on the way, and lock the formula away before Dr. Andhera can use it.

Each time Chintu finishes a stage, the game cuts to Dr. Andhera in his control room, watching on his screens. He starts out calm and amused, then turns annoyed, nervous and finally furious. As he gets more worried he puts up more red searchlights, because those are the only thing that can catch an invisible Chintu.

Dr. Andhera's worry meter (shown in the HUD)
😏 CalmWorld 1. "A child? Ignore him."
😠 AnnoyedWorld 2. Sends henchmen.
😰 WorriedWorld 3. Red searchlights.
😡 FuriousWorld 4. Faces Chintu himself.

The meter fills with distance run and formula pages collected. It drops a little when Chintu is hit. Its level decides which hazards appear, so it works as the game's difficulty setting and is part of the story at the same time.

2Characters (drawn in code from the reference pictures)
Hero
🧒 Chintu
Short dark-brown hair, big grin, yellow short-sleeve shirt, orange-red trousers and chunky black shoes. Animations: run (4 frames), jump, fall, slide, stomp, hurt, and a victory pose. While invisible he is drawn as a faint shimmer, and inside red light he becomes a glowing red outline.
Villain
🦹 Dr. Andhera
An original villain: a tall man with a shaved head, a thin curled moustache and red-lens goggles (so he can see invisible people). He wears a black high-collared coat with gold braid. He appears as a talking portrait in cut-scenes and the HUD, with four faces (calm, annoyed, sweating, furious), and as a full figure in the final boss fight.
Enemies
Henchmen and gadgets
Goons in black and gold who walk and can be stomped. Sentries who stand still and shine red torches. Drones with red lamps that sweep the sky. Barrel-rollers on slopes.

Both characters will be original cartoon drawings made with canvas shapes, Chintu is inspired by the colours and features of the reference picture, which is not copied into the game because it is a watermarked stock image. Dr. Andhera has his own new design and is not based on the villain reference picture, which is a portrait of a real actor.

3How it plays
  • Player-controlled running (changed 21 Nov 2026 after phone testing; it was auto-run). Chintu stands still until RUN (→/D) is held, and BACK (←/A) walks him back. Phones have BACK, RUN and JUMP buttons, with no SLIDE button. Low roofs can be crossed by jumping on top; keyboard players can still slide under with ↓.
  • Jump physics in the classic platformer style. Hold to jump higher, a short "coyote time" after running off a ledge, and a jump buffer so that pressing just before landing still counts.
  • Double jump is unlocked in World 2.
  • Slide under low banners, washing lines and laser beams.
  • Stomp goons by landing on top of them. Running into them from the side costs a heart.
  • Bump blocks from below with a "?" on them to release laddoo coins or power-ups.
  • 3 hearts. A hit removes one heart and gives 1.5 s of flashing safety. Falling into a gap costs one life and sends Chintu back to the last checkpoint flag.
  • Formula pages. There are 3 hidden pages in every stage, and they are the main collectible. Collecting all pages in a world unlocks a bonus stage.
  • Invisibility vial (the signature power-up). For 8 seconds Chintu passes through goons and sentries and they cannot see him. Red light cancels it: inside a red beam he is visible and can be hurt, and some red beams set off alarms that drop cages.
  • Score comes from coins, stomps, pages, time left and a "never seen" bonus for getting through red-light zones without being spotted.

7Technical design
Game loop
Fixed 1/60 s step
The same accumulator pattern as Car Race: update(dt) runs at a fixed rate and render() runs once per frame. The game pauses when the tab is hidden.
Rendering
Low-res canvas, scaled
Draws at 480 × 270, then scales up by a whole number with smoothing off, so the art looks crisp and retro. Uses 3–4 parallax layers. Red light is drawn as a transparent beam with globalCompositeOperation.
Physics
Tile AABB collision
Moves on the X axis and then the Y axis against solid tiles. One-way platforms. Enemies use simple state machines (walk, turn, stunned, squashed).
Red-light check
Point-in-cone test
Each frame, every red beam (a triangle or cone) is tested against Chintu's box. Inside a beam: visible = true, and invisibility does not protect him there.
Saving
localStorage, guarded
Saves the furthest stage unlocked, best scores and pages found. Every read and write is wrapped in try/catch so the game still works in a private window.
Input
Keys, touch, gamepad
Space/↑/W jump · ↓/S slide · Shift/X drink a vial · P/Esc pause. Uses event.code and clears all keys when the window loses focus.

Build phases

Phase 1: Running Chintu (core engine) ✅ done 21 Nov 2026

  • Canvas setup, scaling, fixed-step loop, input handling, pause on hidden tab.
  • Tile map loader for text levels; camera that follows Chintu with some look-ahead.
  • Chintu drawn in code with run, jump and slide animations; auto-run; jump with coyote time and buffering.
  • One test stage made of ground, gaps and blocks; falling into a gap restarts the stage.
Done when: Chintu runs through a test stage at 60 fps, and jumps feel tight and fair with both keyboard and touch.

Phase 2: Hurdles, enemies and pickups

  • Hazards: potholes, carts, cows, low banners, rolling fruit, moving platforms.
  • Goons with stomp and side-hit rules; hearts, flashing safety time, checkpoint flags.
  • Laddoo coins, "?" blocks, formula pages, score and HUD.
  • World 1 (3 stages) and World 2 (3 stages) built as text maps.
Done when: Worlds 1 and 2 can be played from start to finish, with a stage-clear screen and score.

Phase 3: Dr. Andhera, invisibility and red light

  • Dr. Andhera portrait with 4 moods; worry meter in the HUD; short cut-scenes between stages (2–3 lines of text, which can be skipped).
  • Invisibility vial power-up with the shimmer effect and a countdown ring.
  • Red searchlights, sentries and red-lamp drones; the red-outline "revealed" effect; alarms and dropping cages.
  • World 3 (rooftops at night) built around these mechanics. "Never seen" bonus.
Done when: A new player understands within one stage, without reading anything, that red light shows Chintu while he is invisible.

Phase 4: The fortress and the boss

  • World 4: conveyors, laser tripwires and timed red alarms.
  • Boss fight in three rounds (4 lamps; the barrels get faster each round), the vault and the ending cut-scene, then a credits screen.
  • Title screen, stage select map with lock and unlock, and a results screen with stars.
Done when: The whole game can be finished in about 20–25 minutes, and the ending plays.

Phase 5: Sound, polish and phones

  • Web Audio sound effects and an optional chiptune loop; a mute button that is remembered.
  • Screen shake, dust puffs, coin sparkles and a falling formula-page animation.
  • Portrait phone layout with a control deck below the game and an always-visible Stop button; landscape layout with full-screen tap zones.
  • An "Easy" option with 5 hearts and slower speed, for younger players.
Done when: The game is playable at 30+ fps on a phone, with no sound before the first tap and no stuck input.

Phase 6: Portal and explainer

  • Add a Chintu card to GAMES/index.html next to Car Race.
  • info/HOW-CHINTU-WORKS.html: how the levels, physics and red-light check work, and what Claude Code built.
Done when: The game opens from the portal, and the explainer links back to both the game and the portal.

9Test checklist
  • Opens from file:/// in Chrome, Edge and Firefox with no console errors.
  • No getting stuck in walls or falling through floors, including at high speed and on moving platforms.
  • A stomp always counts as a stomp when Chintu is falling onto a goon, never as a hit.
  • Inside red light, invisibility never protects Chintu; outside it, it always does.
  • Every stage can be finished without the double jump, except where the stage design says otherwise.
  • Switching tabs pauses the game; there are no stuck keys afterwards.
  • The game still works in a private window (localStorage blocked).
  • Rotating a phone keeps the canvas sharp, and the controls never cover the play area.
10Decisions (agreed 21 Nov 2026)
  • Art style: drawn in code. Chintu and Dr. Andhera are original cartoon characters drawn with canvas shapes, and the game uses no image files. Chintu is based on the colours in the reference picture. The picture itself is a watermarked stock image, so it is not used.
  • Villain: renamed to Dr. Andhera ("darkness"; working name, easy to change). He has his own new look and lines. The invisibility formula and the red-light weakness stay as the heart of the story.
  • Version 1 scope: Phases 1–3. Worlds 1–3 (village, bazaar, rooftops) with the worry meter, the invisibility vial and red light. The fortress, boss and ending (Phase 4), polish (Phase 5) and the portal card (Phase 6) follow.
  • Language: English with Hindi flavour. Cut-scenes and the villain's lines are in English, with short Hindi exclamations for fun (for example, "Arre!" or "Bas karo!").
  • Genre, not a copy. The game uses the familiar jump-and-run mechanics (blocks, stomping, flags), but has its own characters, world, tiles and sounds. No characters, level layouts, music or artwork from any existing game or film are used.
