How to play

  • Aim: press and drag anywhere on the table; the cue turns to point at your finger or mouse. Use ◀ ▶ for fine aim.
  • Read the guide: the dotted line runs to the first ball; the ring is where your ball will touch it (the ghost ball), the yellow line is where that ball will go, and the faint line is where your cue ball goes after.
  • Set the Power and press SHOOT.
  • Ball in hand: when your white is off the table it comes back into the D (the half-circle on the left); drag it anywhere inside the D. You must then play out of baulk: you may not hit a ball that is in baulk (left of the line) until your ball has crossed the line.
  • Practice table: no rules; drag any ball anywhere, and Cue switches the cue ball.

Scoring

  • Red pot 3 · in-off red 3 (your ball goes in after hitting the red first).
  • Pot of the other white/yellow 2 · in-off it 2 · cannon 2 (your ball hits both other balls).
  • Scores in one shot add up (up to 10). Keep playing while you score; the run is your break.
  • Fouls give points to the other player: missing both balls 2, going in-off without hitting a ball 3, hitting a ball in baulk from hand 2.
  • A potted red goes back on its spot. A potted cue ball stays off until its owner's turn, then is played from the D.
