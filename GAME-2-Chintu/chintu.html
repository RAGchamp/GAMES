What is this and how does AI play a part?

This is Game 2: Chintu, a running and jumping game written by Claude Code (an AI coding agent) as one HTML file with plain JavaScript. Chintu, the goons and everything around them are drawn with code, with no image files. This version is Phase 2 of the development plan: two worlds with six stages, goons, hurdles, laddoos and formula pages. You don't need any AI to play.

How to play

  • Run: hold → or D (RUN on phones). Go back: hold ← or A (BACK on phones).
  • Jump: Space, ↑ or W (JUMP on phones). Hold it to jump higher. In World 2, press it again in the air for a double jump.
  • Goons: Dr. Andhera's men in red goggles. Jump on their heads to stomp them. Touching them from the side costs a heart.
  • Hurdles: jump over potholes, sleeping cows, carts and rolling coconuts. Bounce high on the striped awnings, and ride the flying carpets across wide gaps.
  • Collect: laddoos (100 give an extra life), hearts from blocks, and the 3 formula pages hidden in every stage. Bump ? blocks from below.
  • Hearts and lives: 3 hearts per life. Losing all hearts or falling into a gap costs a life, and you carry on from the last checkpoint flag.
  • Score: laddoo 10 · stomp 100 · formula page 500 · time bonus and heart bonus at the finish gate.
