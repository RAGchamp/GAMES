What is this and how does AI play a part?

This is Game 2: Chintu, a running and jumping game written by Claude Code (an AI coding agent) as one HTML file with plain JavaScript. Chintu, the goons and everything around them are drawn with code, with no image files. This version is Phase 5 of the development plan: the whole story from the village to Dr. Andhera's island fortress, with four worlds, eleven stages and a final showdown in his formula vault, now with sound effects and music that are also made by code, and an Easy mode for younger players. You don't need any AI to play.

How to play

  • Run: hold → or D (RUN on phones). Go back: hold ← or A (BACK on phones).
  • Jump: Space, ↑ or W (JUMP on phones). Hold it to jump higher. In World 2, press it again in the air for a double jump.
  • Goons: Dr. Andhera's men in red goggles. Jump on their heads to stomp them. Touching them from the side costs a heart.
  • Hurdles: jump over potholes, sleeping cows, carts and rolling coconuts. Bounce high on the striped awnings, and ride the flying carpets across wide gaps.
  • Collect: laddoos (100 give an extra life), hearts from blocks, and the 3 formula pages hidden in every stage. Bump ? blocks from below.
  • Hearts and lives: 3 hearts per life. Losing all hearts or falling into a gap costs a life, and you carry on from the last checkpoint flag.
  • Invisibility (World 3): drink a blue vial to vanish for 8 seconds. Goons and white searchlights cannot see you, and you pass straight through goons. But red light shows the invisible: red searchlights, drones with red lamps and sentries with red torches see you either way.
  • Spotted? An alarm rings and a cage drops where you are standing. Keep moving! Finish a rooftop stage without being seen for a Never seen bonus.
  • The fortress (World 4): conveyor belts carry you along (or push you back). Jump over red tripwires or the alarm goes off. Laser gates hurt even when you are invisible: wait until they switch off. Red alarm lamps flash on and off, so cross while they are dark.
  • The final showdown: Dr. Andhera watches his vault through 4 big red lamps and throws barrels at anyone he can see. Drink a vial so he can only see you inside the red light, and jump on the switch under each lamp to break it. With all 4 lamps broken he can't see a thing: climb up and lock the formula away in the vault.
  • Stars: every stage gives up to 3 stars: one for finishing, one for all 3 formula pages (in the vault: for taking no more than 2 hits) and one for beating the target time.
  • Dr. Andhera's worry meter fills as you get closer to his formula. Watch his face change from calm to annoyed, worried and furious.
  • Sound: every sound and all the music are made by the game's code (no sound files). Switch sound or music off on the menus, in the pause menu or with the buttons under the game, or press M to mute. The choice is remembered.
  • Easy mode (on the title screen and the stage map): 5 hearts instead of 3, a slightly slower game and a little longer safety time after a hit. Great for younger players.
  • Gamepad: left stick or d-pad to run, A or B to jump, down to slide, Start to pause. A also works the menus.
  • Score: laddoo 10 · stomp 100 · formula page 500 · time bonus and heart bonus at the finish gate.
