How to play

  • Pick a club: the Driver goes furthest (only from the tee), the Wedge goes high and short, the Putter rolls the ball on the green.
  • Aim with ◀ ▶ (or drag on the map in top-down view). Each shot starts aimed for you.
  • Set the Angle (how high) and the Power, then press SWING.
  • ⇄ View switches between the side view (hills and height) and the top-down view (bends, trees and hazards) at any time.

The course

  • 18 holes, par 72. Par is the number of strokes a good golfer needs. One under is a birdie, one over a bogey.
  • Rough and bunkers slow the ball and weaken your next shot. Water and out of bounds cost a penalty stroke.
  • Wind: a new wind on every hole, and on a long hole it changes every 3 minutes. Crosswind curves the ball.
  • A ball that stops very close to the cup is tapped in for one more stroke. A hole ends after 10 strokes.

How does AI play a part?
